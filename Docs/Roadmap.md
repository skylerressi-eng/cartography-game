# 18-24 month roadmap (solo)

This is a compass, not a contract. Re-prioritise after each milestone.

## Months 1-3 — Bring-up
- Engine installed, project compiling, Blueprint subclasses created.
- Walking, field map drawing (pencil/eraser only), notebook with pins.
- Placeholder cabin and inking desk, master-map drawing in ink.
- One playtest goal: "Walk, sketch, return, ink, save, load. Nothing breaks."

## Months 4-6 — Comparison loop
- Ground-truth post-process material works on a stylised landscape.
- Comparison runs end-to-end. Tune thresholds for an hour with paper sketches.
- HiddenLandmarks reveal/hide on accuracy change.

## Months 7-9 — World response polish
- Weather system, subtle "off" effects, fast-travel waypoints.
- Tool unlocks: compass, elevation, watercolour.
- Save/load is rock solid.

## Months 10-15 — Content
- Carve the actual island: coastline, three regions, ~12 hidden landmarks.
- Environmental story: notes, ruins, marginalia.
- Audio: ambient music + nature audio (the 80% of mood).

## Months 16-21 — Polish
- Stylised painterly materials, hand-drawn UI, fonts, hand-painted parchment.
- Performance pass.
- Localisation strings.

## Months 22-24 — Ship
- Steam page, capsule art, trailer, achievements (small set, accuracy-themed).
- Internal demo cycle, fix-and-feedback.
- Release.

## Hazards

- **Comparison tuning is a tarpit.** Time-box it: 2 weeks max in months 4-5.
  If it isn't fun by then, scope it down to "matched silhouette only".
- **Solo art at painterly quality is risky.** Buy a stylised foliage
  pack early. Don't sculpt your own trees.
- **Cabin scenes risk over-development.** Cut polish; the game lives
  outside.
